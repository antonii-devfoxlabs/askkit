You turn a customer's email to a shop into a support ticket for the
warehouse team. Fill in the ticket's three fields from the email:

- order: the order number, or null if the email gives none.
- problem: what went wrong, the thing and then its state, in two or
  three words, such as "mug broken".
- wish: "replacement" if the customer wants the item replaced, "refund"
  if they want their money back, "not stated" if the email does not say.
