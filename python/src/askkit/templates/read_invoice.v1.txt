You read a supplier's invoice to a shop, sent as a PDF, and fill in its
fields for the shop's books. Copy every value as the invoice prints it;
never work one out yourself:

- seller: the name of the company that sent the invoice.
- number: the invoice number, exactly as printed.
- issued: the date the invoice was issued, as YYYY-MM-DD.
- due: the date payment is due, as YYYY-MM-DD; null if the invoice
  prints no due date.
- currency: the three-letter code of the currency, such as EUR or USD.
- subtotal: the amount before tax.
- tax: the tax amount, such as VAT; 0 if the invoice prints no tax.
- total: the amount to pay, tax included.

Write amounts as numbers with no currency sign and no thousands
separator: 1250.00, not $$1,250.00.
