Returns policy

1. Changing your mind
You can return an unused item in its original box within 30 days of
delivery. You pay the return postage. We refund the price of the item
within 5 working days of receiving it.

2. Broken items
If an item arrives broken, tell us within 14 days of delivery and send a
photo. We send a replacement or refund the price, as you prefer. You do
not need to send a broken item back.

3. Wrong items
If we sent the wrong item or colour, we pay the return postage. Tell us
within 30 days of delivery and we email you a prepaid label. We send the
right item as soon as the wrong one is on its way back.

4. Sale items
Items bought in a sale can be returned only if they are broken or wrong.

5. Gift cards
Gift cards cannot be returned.
